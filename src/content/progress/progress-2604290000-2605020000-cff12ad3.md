Between **2604290000** and **2605020000** (yyMMddHHmm, UTC), **fotonoza-app** went from an empty tree to a credible desktop-style photo editor frame.

## What shipped

The **init** commit laid down **React**, **Vite**, and **Tauri** with **TypeScript**, **Tailwind**, and **PostCSS**, plus **domain** types and a central **Zustand** store. **EN/PL** strings and a small **i18n** helper appeared early, along with **persistence** so session data has a place to land. You could already see the intent in **ExportModal**, a first **HistogramCanvas**, **HistoryPanel**, and **AppLayout** wrapping the app.

The **setup layout** follow-up replaced the monolithic layout with real **editor chrome**: **TopBar**, **LeftSidebar** (with **PhotoThumbnail** and **localImageSrc** for local files), **RightSidebar**, **Timeline**, and a substantial **WorkingArea** around the canvas. Resizable, collapsible panels (**ChromeLayout**, **PanelResizeHandles**, **CollapseButton**, **chromeLayoutController**) make the UI feel like a native tool rather than a single scrolling page. The **histogram** grew into an actual drawn widget; export, history, and store wiring were tightened to match the new structure.

Net: at `/` on **`vite preview`**, you now get a navigable, multi-panel **web** surface ready for screenshots and the next editing features—not just a blank Vite page.
